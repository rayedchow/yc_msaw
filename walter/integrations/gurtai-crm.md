# GurtAI CRM (HubSpot-style)
Sales system of record: deals, contacts, companies, owners and pipelines.

## Connection
- REST under /crm/v3 (objects, search, pipelines, owners, properties)
- Bearer token, injected by the host: GURTAI_TOKEN

## Notes
- Enterprise and Partner Sandbox pipelines live next to the default one
- RevOps (owner 204) maintains the books
