# Gmail · support@gurtai.example
The shared support inbox: customer emails, billing questions and automated notifications.

## Connection
- Gmail API style under /mail/gmail/v1/users/me (threads, labels; q= search)
- Bearer token, injected by the host: GURTAI_TOKEN

## Notes
- Queue labels are applied by whoever is on triage duty
- Support, Finance and AR people all work this inbox
